## The Great Sin (1905) — the copyist

The identity was checked first, since the dive routed a copyist named only «М. Л. Оболенская» and the vault holds more than one Maria. It is the daughter: the existing live page is Maria Lvovna (m. Obolenskaya, 1871–1906), Tolstoy's daughter — not the sister Maria Nikolaevna. Confirmed while loose-matching the vault, 2026-07-27.

On 1 July 1905, per [[Dushan Makovitsky]]'s notes, Tolstoy gave the finished [[The Great Sin]] to Maria Lvovna to copy out for *Русская мысль*, and meant to take it to the post himself. Her copy has not survived, and the <abbr title="Полное собрание сочинений — the 90-volume Jubilee edition of the complete works">PSS</abbr> commentary uses that fact to explain why the journal text diverges in places from the surviving manuscripts: those differences may be small corrections Tolstoy made *in her copy* — the one witness now lost. So her transcription sits, invisibly, behind the first published Russian text.
