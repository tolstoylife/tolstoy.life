## The Great Sin (1905) — the copyist

The copyist the <abbr title="Полное собрание сочинений — the 90-volume Jubilee edition of the complete works">PSS</abbr> commentary names as M. L. Obolenskaya (<span lang="ru">М. Л. Оболенская</span>) is the daughter Maria Lvovna, not the sister Maria Nikolaevna; the commentary itself names her as the daughter.[^1]

On 1 July 1905, by [[Dushan Makovitsky]]'s notes, the finished [[The Great Sin]] was given to her to copy for [[Russkaya Mysl]], with the plan of taking it to the post in person.[^1] Her copy has not survived. The commentary uses the lost copy to explain why the journal text differs in places from the surviving manuscripts: the differences may be corrections made in her copy.[^2]
