Samara Governorate (Самарская губерния) appears in [[Confession]] (Исповедь, 1879–82) twice: as the steppe of the 1862 kumys stay, and as the estate behind the question «а потом?..» — "and what then?". A draft adds the Molokans of Samara.

## The 1862 kumys stay

Chapter III tells of a year of arbitration work, the schools and the magazine, ending in exhaustion: «я заболел более духовно, чем физически, — бросил всё и поехал в степь к башкирам — дышать воздухом, пить кумыс и жить животною жизнью» — in Maude's words, "I fell ill, mentally rather than physically, threw up everything, and went away to the Bashkirs in the steppes, to breathe fresh air, drink kumys, and live a merely animal life" (<abbr title="Полное собрание сочинений — the 90-volume Jubilee edition of the complete works">PSS</abbr> 23:10). Kumys, Maude's note explains, is "a fermented drink prepared from mare's milk".

The PSS commentary adds the details: the first journey for kumys to Samara Governorate, begun 26 May 1862 (OS: 14 May), with two pupils of the Yasnaya Polyana school, Vasily Morozov and Egor Chernov. He lived in a Bashkir nomad camp on the Karalyk, in Nikolaevsky uezd (district) of the governorate, and was back at [[Yasnaya Polyana]] on 12 August 1862 (OS: 31 July). Morozov later wrote down his memories of the stay, published by Posrednik in 1917 (PSS 23, commentary note 19).

## The estate and "what then?"

In chapter III the estate comes first among the occupations that now needed a reason: «Прежде чем заняться самарским имением, воспитанием сына, писанием книги, надо знать, зачем я это буду делать» — "occupying myself with my Samara estate, the education of my son, or the writing of a book, I had to know *why* I was doing it" (Maude). Then the question: «Ну хорошо, у тебя будет 6000 десятин в Самарской губернии, 300 голов лошадей, а потом?..» — "Well, you will have 6,000 desyatinas of land in Samara Government and 300 horses, and what then?" (PSS 23, ch. III; Maude). A desyatina, Maude notes, is about 2¾ acres.

The figures are in the future tense («у тебя будет», "you will have"). When the estate was bought, and its size: NEEDS PRIMARY SOURCE.

## The Molokans in a draft

A draft of chapter XV (PSS variant No. 20), on the schismatics, names Samara: «В Самаре, где я жил, много молокан» — "In Samara, where I lived, there are many Molokans" (working English). It recalls one old Molokan «который роздал всё свое имение неимущим и из богатого стал бедным, только для того, чтобы последовать правилу: просящему дай» — "who gave away all his property to the needy and from rich became poor, only to follow the rule: give to him that asks" (working English) (PSS 23:488–511, variant No. 20; page not pinned). The final chapter XV keeps the Molokans only in a general list — «католиками, протестантами, старообрядцами, молоканами и др.» ("Catholics, Protestants, Old Believers, Molokans and others", working English) — with no Samara and no old man (PSS 23, ch. XV).
